FAQ

Things people
ask Pop.

How is Popodoro different from a regular pomodoro timer?

Most timers wait for you. Pop watches your patterns — when you actually finish sessions vs. when you bail — and surfaces those windows as soft nudges. Same 25-minute timer, more useful around it.

Does Popodoro track me?

No. There are no third-party trackers, no analytics SDKs, no ad networks. Your sessions live on your device. If you turn on sync, it's end-to-end encrypted — we cannot see your tasks, tags, or times.

Can I use Popodoro without an account?

Yes. The free tier is local-only and never asks for an email. You only sign up if you want sync, Plus features, or to invite buddies by handle.

What's a "golden hour"?

A repeating time window where you historically finish what you start. Pop calculates them from your last ~30 sessions. You'll see your golden hours on the Stats page after about a week of use.

How do I cancel or delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. We purge everything within 7 days. There's also a direct link in the footer that doesn't require signing in.

Is there a Linux version?

Yes — .deb, .rpm, AppImage, and a Flatpak. We test on Ubuntu, Fedora, and Arch. There's also an unofficial AUR build the community maintains.
